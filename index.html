Working paper • Version 1.0

Controlling Autonomous AI

How can an organisation retain meaningful control when AI agents are given authority to move money, alter systems, change records and instruct other agents?

The paper develops the ideas of Control Illusion, Control Margin and global admissibility and proposes IABEI — Identity, Authority, Boundaries, Evidence and Intervention — as an assurance structure for autonomous action.
